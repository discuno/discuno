## Why Peer Mentorship Matters

At Discuno, we believe firsthand student experience can add useful context to a college decision. Mentors verify access to a school-issued email address, then describe the subjects and situations they can responsibly speak to.

Peer mentorship conversations can offer:

- A practical perspective alongside official resources
- Questions you may not know to ask yet
- A chance to compare possible next steps
- Encouragement from someone closer to the experience
