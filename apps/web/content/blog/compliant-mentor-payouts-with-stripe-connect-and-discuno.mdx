Paying mentors sounds simple—until you factor in identity checks, cancellation windows, refunds, and disputes. Discuno uses Stripe Connect for connected-account onboarding and keeps payment state tied to each booking. This guide explains the current workflow; it is not legal, tax, or accounting advice.

## Why Payments Are Tricky for Mentorship Programs

- **Multiple funding models:** Sessions may be paid by mentees, subsidized by grants, or comped for scholarships.
- **Variable rates:** Mentors set different prices, currencies, and durations that change over time.
- **Staggered payouts:** Programs often delay mentor payouts until mentees confirm the session delivered value.
- **Regulatory complexity:** Tax reporting, KYC requirements, and regional banking rules vary by mentor location.

Discuno abstracts this complexity so you can focus on delivering outcomes, not solving fintech puzzles.

## Discuno + Stripe Connect Workflow

1. **Mentor onboarding:** Mentors complete Stripe Connect onboarding directly from the Discuno dashboard. Stripe handles identity verification, bank account linking, and tax form collection.
2. **Checkout:** Mentees pay the listed session price plus applicable tax through Stripe Checkout. Discuno adds no buyer service fee and retains a 15% mentor-side commission.
3. **Ledger reconciliation:** Discuno’s Drizzle ORM schema stores payment, mentor, and booking metadata in normalized tables (`discuno_payment`, `discuno_booking`, `discuno_mentor_stripe_account`).
4. **Support window:** The mentor’s 85% share remains on the platform until 72 hours after the scheduled session ends.
5. **Policy-gated transfer:** Inngest releases that share only when the booking was delivered, the mentee did not attend, or a late mentee cancellation remains payout-eligible—and only when the payment is not refunded, disputed, or manually held.
6. **Reconciliation:** Stripe IDs and lifecycle statuses are stored with the booking so operators can reconcile activity in Stripe and Inngest.

## Handling Refunds and Adjustments

- **Clear launch policy:** Mentor cancellations, mentor no-shows, and mentee cancellations at least 24 hours ahead receive a full refund. A mentee cancellation less than 24 hours ahead is non-refundable, and the mentor's 85% share remains eligible after the scheduled session end plus 72 hours.
- **Idempotent operations:** Repeated webhook delivery cannot create duplicate refunds or mentor transfers.
- **Reconciliation:** Refund IDs, status, amount, and transfer reversals are retained with the payment record.

## Compliance and Risk Mitigation

- **Connected-account onboarding:** Stripe collects the identity and bank details required for each connected account’s enabled capabilities.
- **Platform controls:** Discuno checks booking ownership and Better Auth permissions before mentor-side cancellation actions.
- **Webhook integrity:** Stripe and Cal.com signatures are verified before local payment or booking state changes.
- **Professional advice still matters:** Marketplace, tax, and reporting obligations depend on your business and operating regions.

## Operational Best Practices

- **Reconcile weekly:** Compare Discuno payment records with Stripe charges, refunds, disputes, and transfers.
- **Communicate timelines:** Publish payout schedules and dispute policies to mentors, so expectations stay clear.
- **Review alerts:** Investigate failed refunds, transfer reversals, and Stripe disputes promptly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is a mentor transfer released?**

The mentor’s 85% share becomes eligible 72 hours after the scheduled session ends, subject to booking delivery and payment review.

**How do we manage chargebacks?**

Discuno blocks an unreleased mentor transfer, records the dispute state, and alerts operators. Evidence and case responses are managed in Stripe.
