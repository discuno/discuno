Starting at the University of Michigan is exciting, intimidating, and full of decisions. A current student who has navigated Ann Arbor can add useful firsthand context to the official information you receive from the university. Use this guide to prepare focused questions before move-in day.

## Why Talk to a Discuno Mentor First?

- **Firsthand perspective:** Ask how one student experienced class rigor, campus culture, and different parts of Ann Arbor.
- **Faster decisions:** Mentors help you prioritize housing, student orgs, and internship prep so you avoid choice overload.
- **Relevant context:** Read each mentor’s school, major, and profile to judge whether their experience fits your question.

## Essential Topics to Cover

### Academics

- Which introductory courses fill up fastest?
- What do professors actually expect in first-year seminars?
- How do you balance lectures, labs, and discussion sections without burning out?

### Campus Life

- Where should first-years live if they want a quieter dorm?
- Which student orgs help you meet friends quickly?
- What are the underrated traditions every Wolverine should know?

### Career Prep

- How early should you visit the career center or attend companies’ info sessions?
- What does a successful freshman-year resume look like?
- Which local internships or research labs are open to first-year students?

### Financial Wellness

- How do meal plans compare to cooking in off-campus apartments?
- What are the go-to resources for scholarships, emergency funds, and part-time jobs?
- Are there hidden costs (lab fees, course packets, transportation) to budget for?

## Sample Conversation Starter

```text

```

## Bonus: Ann Arbor Starting Points to Ask About

- Study spots: Hatcher Graduate Library stacks, Michigan Union lounges, or the top floor of the Biological Sciences building.
- Food: Frita Batidos, Zingerman’s Roadhouse, and late-night Joe’s Pizza.
- Outdoor escapes: Nichols Arboretum for fall colors, Gallup Park when you need a quick reset.
